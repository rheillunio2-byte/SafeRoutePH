KNOW WHAT TO DO

Disaster preparedness guides

Basic safety reminders for common hazards.

〰
FLOODING

When floodwaters rise

Move to higher ground when instructed. Avoid walking or driving through floodwater.

  • Monitor official weather and flood advisories.
  • Move electrical appliances and important documents to higher places when safe.
  • Evacuate early when authorities advise it.
Check PAGASA flood information ↗
⌁
EARTHQUAKE

During ground shaking

Protect yourself using Drop, Cover, and Hold On.

  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  • Do not use elevators during or immediately after shaking.
  • Expect possible aftershocks and follow official instructions.
Visit PHIVOLCS ↗
☁
TROPICAL CYCLONE

Before a strong storm

Prepare your household and stay updated through official weather bulletins.

  • Secure loose outdoor objects before strong winds arrive.
  • Charge phones and prepare emergency supplies.
  • Follow evacuation orders and avoid exposed areas.
