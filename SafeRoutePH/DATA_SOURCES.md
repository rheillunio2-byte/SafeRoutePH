## Data Verification Policy

Before adding any evacuation center or emergency facility, verify:

1. Official designation by the responsible authority.
2. Exact location and coordinates.
3. Capacity and accessibility.
4. Hazards the facility is intended to accommodate.
5. Current operational status.
6. Date of the latest verification.

Unverified facilities must not be presented as official evacuation centers.

## Map Disclaimer

The city-center marker is an approximate orientation reference only. It does not indicate an evacuation center, safe zone, or recommended destination.

## Information Limitations

SafeRoute PH does not currently integrate live government alert feeds, flood sensors, road closures, or emergency dispatch systems.

Users should consult official authorities for current instructions.
